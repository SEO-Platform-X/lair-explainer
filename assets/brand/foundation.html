01The brand

We get local businesses recommended by AI — and prove it in a public record.

What we are

An AI-optimization company — the next chapter of SEO. We move local businesses to the top of the answer when customers ask an assistant who to trust.

What we run

A public directory — the Registry — recording who each business is and what they’re known for: the structured facts an assistant needs to recommend them.

Why it matters

People no longer scroll results — they trust the one name AI gives back. If it isn’t yours, you’re invisible. Our whole identity is built to make it yours.

02The name

A name that sounds like the body you'd already trust.

Local

Yours, near, specific. Rooted in a place and a trade — the opposite of a faceless platform.

AI

Where the decision now happens. We name the arena plainly — no hype, no jargon.

Registry

An authoritative, kept record. The word carries the weight: official, lasting, accountable.

Lead with the full name

Always “Local AI Registry.” In running copy, shorthand is “the Registry.”

No acronym

We never reduce it to initials. The mark — not a set of letters — is our shorthand.

Plain casing

Title case in prose; the wordmark sets its own case. Never all-caps the name in body copy.

03The mark

An ascent — the climb to the top of the answer.

The mark reads two ways at once: an A for AI, and a peak rising to a single point — the top spot in a recommendation. The tip is coral because that summit is the whole job. It holds from billboard to favicon.

The wordmark is set in Bricolage Grotesque, not the display serif. A grotesque holds the name as a stable institution mark — legible at 16px, sturdy in the nav. Cormorant is for statements, not the logotype.

Clear space

Keep space equal to the width of one leg of the mark on every side. Let it breathe.

Minimum size

Mark 16px. Full lockup 120px. Below that, use the mark alone.

Don't

No stretching, no rotating the peak, no recoloring outside the palette, no shadows or gradients.

04Color

Electric blue, deep ink, a coral that means business.

Blue carries the brand and reads as trust. Coral is the signal — used on one thing at a time, never as decoration. Warm paper keeps it human, not clinical.

05Typography

Three voices. One register.

The serif speaks, the grotesque labels, the sans serves. A high-contrast display serif carries the statements; Bricolage handles loud labels and numerals; Hanken does the reading.

06Voice

Established, never stiff. Certain, never cold.

Plain-spoken

Say it the way you'd say it across a counter. Short sentences, real words.

Certain

We state, we don’t hedge. An authority doesn’t say “we think we might help.”

On your side

The reader is the owner. We make them look credible — not us.
